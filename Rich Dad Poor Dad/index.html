The CASHFLOW Quadrant

Which Side Are You On?

Four types of people. The rich operate on the right side (B and I). Click each quadrant to learn more.

E
Employee
I want job security and a steady paycheck.
+
Employees value security and benefits. They trade time for money. When they stop working, the income stops. They are the largest quadrant — and the least financially free.
S
Self-Employed
I want to be my own boss and do things my way.
+
Self-employed own their job. They are the best workers but the worst bosses — they still trade time for money, just on their own terms. They are often "owned by their business."
B
Business Owner
I build systems that work for me, not the other way around.
+
Business owners own a system that works without them. They leverage other people's time and money. A B can leave their business for a year and return to find it more profitable.
I
Investor
I make money work for me. Money works, I don't.
+
Investors make money from investments. They don't work for money — their money works for them. This is the ultimate quadrant of financial freedom. Rich Dad's goal: E → S → B → I.
→ → →
The path to financial freedom: Move from the left side (E and S) to the right side (B and I). The right side is where your money works for you, not the other way around.
The Rat Race

Escaping the Rat Race

The endless cycle of working for money and spending it all — and how to break free.

The rat race is the cycle of working for money, spending it all, and needing more. The only way out: build assets that generate passive income until your asset column exceeds your expenses.
