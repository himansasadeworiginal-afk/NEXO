Communication

How to Talk to Each Type

Click a card to reveal detailed communication strategies for each behavioral type.

D

Communicating with Reds

  • Be direct and concise — get to the point
  • Focus on results and efficiency
  • Give them options and let them decide
  • Don't waste time with small talk
  • Don't be vague or ambiguous

Reds respect competence and brevity. Lead with the bottom line, then provide supporting details if asked. Challenge them respectfully — they respect those who stand their ground. Frame your message in terms of goals, outcomes, and competitive advantage.

Details
I

Communicating with Yellows

  • Be enthusiastic and energetic
  • Show appreciation and recognition
  • Use stories and visual examples
  • Don't bore them with excessive details
  • Don't dismiss their ideas harshly

Yellows thrive on enthusiasm and social connection. Start with the big picture vision, share testimonials or stories, and give them public recognition. They need to feel inspired. Follow up in writing to ensure details aren't lost in the excitement.

Details
S

Communicating with Greens

  • Show genuine care and listen actively
  • Provide stability and clear expectations
  • Give them time to process
  • Don't push for quick decisions
  • Don't be aggressive or confrontational

Greens value trust and relationships above all. Take time to build rapport before getting to business. Ask about their well-being, listen patiently, and explain how changes will affect the team. Reassure them about job security and stability.

Details
C

Communicating with Blues

  • Present data, facts, and evidence
  • Be precise, logical, and well-organized
  • Give them time to analyze thoroughly
  • Don't exaggerate or make emotional appeals
  • Don't rush their decision-making process

Blues are driven by logic and accuracy. Prepare thoroughly before meeting — bring data, citations, and well-structured arguments. Anticipate their questions and address potential flaws proactively. Respect their need for quality and precision.
