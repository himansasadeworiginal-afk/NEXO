## NEXO 2.0 — Full Website Upgrade

You are upgrading NEXO 1.0, a single-file HTML digital library hub (index.html). Below is a complete description of what exists and exactly what I want changed. Do not rewrite things that are not asked to change. Preserve all existing book data, file paths, and core functionality unless explicitly told otherwise.

---

### CONTEXT: What NEXO currently is

NEXO is a self-contained single-file HTML app (no frameworks, no CDN, pure HTML/CSS/JS) that acts as a hub for interactive book summaries and study notes. It has:
- 24 books across 5 categories (Productivity, Philosophy, Psychology, Power, Finance)
- 20 study lessons: 8 Business Studies + 12 Economics
- A dark green terminal-style theme (#060906 background)
- Canvas particle background, floating orbs, mouse glow
- Search + category filter + grid/list toggle
- All 44 resources in one flat book grid

The current problem: books and study lessons are treated equally in one flat grid. Going forward, LESSONS are the primary content and BOOKS are secondary. I also plan to add 12+ more subjects in the future.

---

### GOAL: NEXO 2.0

Transform NEXO into a structured, subject-first learning platform with rich interactivity, especially around lessons. The upgrade has 4 pillars:

1. Architecture restructure (subjects first, books as sub-category)
2. Visual and style upgrade (same dark theme, significantly more polished)
3. Enhanced interactivity and animations
4. Interactive quizzes and tests embedded in the lesson cards

### PILLAR 4 — INTERACTIVE QUIZZES (most important new feature)

Each lesson should have a set of quiz questions. When the user clicks "Quiz" on a lesson card, a modal overlay opens with an interactive quiz.

**Quiz modal design:**
- Full-screen dark overlay (rgba(0,0,0,0.85)) with a centered modal card
- Modal card: max-width 640px, same dark surface style (#0c110c), green accent border
- Header: subject name + lesson title + "Quiz" label
- Progress indicator: "Question 3 of 8" with a thin progress bar
- Close button (X) top right
- Smooth fade-in animation on open, fade-out on close
- ESC key closes the modal

**Quiz question format (multiple choice):**
- Show one question at a time
- 4 answer options as clickable cards/buttons
- On selection: immediately show if correct (green highlight + checkmark) or wrong (red highlight + show correct answer highlighted in green)
- "Next Question" button appears after answering
- After last question: show a results screen with score (e.g. "7 / 8 correct"), performance message, and a "Retake Quiz" / "Close" button
- Store best score per lesson in localStorage: key `nexo_quiz_{subjectId}_{lessonId}_best`
- Show best score badge on the lesson card (e.g. "Best: 7/8") if a score exists

**Quiz data structure (implement as a JS object at the top of the script section):**
Define a `QUIZ_DATA` object keyed by subject and lesson ID. Populate with REAL questions for the following lessons (write actual curriculum-appropriate questions, not placeholders):

  Economics Lesson 1 — Introduction to Economics:
    - 8 questions covering: scarcity, opportunity cost, factors of production, economic systems, needs vs wants, the basic economic problem, PPC curve concepts

  Economics Lesson 2 — Demand, Supply & Market Equilibrium:
    - 8 questions covering: law of demand/supply, demand/supply shifters, equilibrium price, surplus/shortage, price ceiling/floor

  Business Studies Lesson 1 — Basis of Business & Environment:
    - 8 questions covering: business definition, types of business activity, business objectives, stakeholders, PEST factors, business environment

  Business Studies Lesson 2 — Social Responsibility & Business Ethics:
    - 8 questions covering: CSR definition, ethical vs legal, examples of CSR, stakeholder impact, triple bottom line, ethical trading

  For ALL other lessons (Economics 3–12, Business 3–8): add 4 placeholder questions each with the structure:
    { q: "Placeholder question for [lesson name] #N", options: ["A","B","C","D"], answer: 0 }
  with a comment /* TODO: Replace with real questions */ above each placeholder block

**Quiz UX details:**
- Disable clicking other options after one is selected
- Show explanation text (optional field in data: `explain: "..."`) below the correct answer if provided — only the Economics and Business Lesson 1 & 2 questions need explanation text, others can omit
- Keyboard: 1/2/3/4 keys select option A/B/C/D when question is active
